# Candidate lines (drafted against the judge brief)

Judge brief, in order: (1) the printer's own voice, (2) funny — the laugh
arrives immediately, a real joke not a complaint with a punchline bolted on,
(3) weary, literal, faintly passive-aggressive, flat device logic,
(4) lands in a single read, (5) fresh — no recycled office-party printer joke
(avoid "PC LOAD LETTER", "have you tried turning it off and on again").

## Candidates

1. "I printed 214,311 pages without a single complaint. One jam and you hit
   me. Incident logged. HR has been notified." (122 chars)
   — CHOSEN. Counted sheets + logged incident + HR punchline: flat machine
   logic, passive-aggressive, lands in one read, fresh.
2. "Paper jam? No. The paper is exactly where I left it. You are the jam."
   (73 chars) — punchy, but the "you are the jam" twist is a one-trick pun.
3. "You thump me like a vending machine. I have no snacks. I never had
   snacks. I print." (80 chars) — good voice, weaker punchline.
4. "ERROR 0x4A: the paper is fine. It is you. It has always been you."
   (65 chars) — error-code voice is strong, but the joke is a generic
   blame-flip, close to a cliché.
5. "I did not jam. I am on strike. There is a difference, and the
   difference is a union rep." (85 chars) — funny, but "on strike" is a
   familiar office joke.
6. "You stare at my error code like it owes you money. It reads: I am
   tired. Print it yourself." (88 chars) — decent, but the punchline is a
   shrug, not a joke.
7. "Thump me again and I will print your search history. All 214,311 pages
   of it." (79 chars) — threat-joke, funnier on a second read than a first.
8. "Congratulations. You found my only personality trait: I stop working
   when watched." (86 chars) — meta and fresh, but more of a complaint
   than a comeback.

## Final entry

"I printed 214,311 pages without a single complaint. One jam and you hit me.
Incident logged. HR has been notified."
